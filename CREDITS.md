# Credits and Attributions

Noisemaker's shader effects build on work generously shared by the creative coding and graphics communities. The following external works are used or referenced in Noisemaker's shader source, with gratitude to their authors.

- [Bit fields](https://twitter.com/aemkei/status/1378106731386040322) — inspiration from @aemkei
- [CGA and bloom effects](https://github.com/spite/Wagner) by Jasper St. Pierre (Spite)
- [Dark Eclipse fractals](https://github.com/darkeclipz/fractals) — fractal shader reference
- [glfx.js zoom blur](https://github.com/evanw/glfx.js) by Evan Wallace
- [glsl-voronoi-noise](https://github.com/MaxBittker/glsl-voronoi-noise) by Max Bittker
- [Julia fractal reference](http://nuclear.mutantstargoat.com/articles/sdr_fract/) by Nuclear / Mutant Stargoat
- [Newton fractal shader](https://github.com/rupak10987/Shaders) by Rupak
- [Oklab colorspace](https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/) by [Björn Ottosson](https://bottosson.github.io/)
- [PCG prng](https://github.com/riccardoscalco/glsl-pcg-prng) by Riccardo Scalco
- [Simplex, cell noise](https://github.com/ashima/webgl-noise/) by Ian McEwan, Ashima Arts (maintained by Stefan Gustavson)
- [Smooth minimum, 3D SDFs](https://www.iquilezles.org/www/index.htm) by Inigo Quilez
- [Tunnel shader](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Ms2SWW) by Inigo Quilez
